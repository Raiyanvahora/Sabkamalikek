About Us

About Sabka Malik Ek Tours

Sabka Malik Ek Tours is a chauffeur-driven Tempo Traveller rental service based in Anand, Gujarat, offering safe and comfortable travel for families, groups and corporates.

We provide vehicle rental with a professional, experienced driver only — no self-drive option — so you can relax and enjoy the journey while we take care of the road. From local trips across Anand, Nadiad and Charotar to religious pilgrimages and all-India tours, our well-maintained fleet of 12-Seater Maharaja, 16-Seater Urbania, 17 and 20-seater Tempo Travellers is ready for every occasion.

  • Chauffeur-driven only — professional drivers on every trip
  • 4 vehicle options to match your group size
  • Local expertise across Anand, Nadiad & Charotar
  • Available for Gujarat & all-India outstation tours
